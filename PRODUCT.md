# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who listen while they work. They need a compact desktop player that stays easy to control without competing with the task in front of them.

## Product Purpose

G Music is a focused, keyboard-first desktop music player. It presents the current track beautifully, keeps transport and queue control immediate, and stays compact during long work sessions.

## Brand Personality

Focused, beautiful, modern. The product feels intentional and calm, with enough visual character to make listening feel considered rather than incidental.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic media-player chrome, dashboard-like card layouts, loud gamer styling, fake physical hardware, and decorative effects that distract from work.

## Design Principles

- Make the now-playing surface feel like a refined listening object, not a utility panel.
- Keep the current track, transport controls, and progress clear at a glance.
- Give artwork and playback state visual presence without adding noise.
- Preserve fast keyboard and pointer control for queue management.
- Use visual restraint to support long, focused work sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet industry-standard accessibility practices. Support keyboard operation, screen readers, clear focus indication, readable contrast, and reduced motion preferences.
